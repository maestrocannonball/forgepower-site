On April 28, 2025, the Iberian Peninsula experienced a massive power outage that
plunged Spain, Portugal, and parts of France into darkness. This unprecedented
event disrupted transportation, healthcare, communications, and industry,
highlighting the vulnerabilities of modern power grids and the urgent need for
resilient energy infrastructure.

The Iberian Peninsula blackout was a stark reminder of how fragile our modern
grid really is. One failure cascaded into another, and before long, major cities
across Spain and Portugal were plunged into darkness. It wasn't a freak
accident — it was a systemic vulnerability. And it won't be the last.
